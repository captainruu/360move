About 360 Move

A training ground built around every angle of fitness.

360 MOVE Gym & Training Center is a fitness studio in Uluwatu built for every level — from first-timers to competitive surfers. Between HIIT floors, a hot pilates studio, and an open gym stocked with indoor and outdoor equipment, the space is designed so your training never has to compromise.

Beyond the workout: on-site physiotherapy, showers, lockers, and a proper changing area — so a session here fits into a real day, not around it.
